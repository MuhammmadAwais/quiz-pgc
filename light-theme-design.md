## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, high-clarity aesthetic engineered specifically for high-end digital applications, presentation dashboards, and environments requiring razor-sharp legibility (including ambient daylight and conference projection displays).

The visual personality combines the structural authority of deep royal indigo with the luminous optimism of periwinkle blue and ice mist. By strictly rejecting generic chromatic accents in favor of a cohesive monochromatic and analogous blue-lavender range, the system evokes poise, architectural precision, and uncompromising quality. 

The design language synthesizes modern corporate clarity with crisp soft-edge geometry. Surfaces are pure, layered, and buoyant, rejecting muddy drop shadows in favor of ambient cool-toned periwinkle diffusion and distinct border delineations.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from an architectural cascade of royal indigo, soft mist, and crystalline periwinkle tints. Every tone fulfills an exacting functional role to ensure WCAG AAA compliance and projection clarity:

- **Primary (`#3D52A0` - Deep Royal Indigo):** Anchors high-priority actions, primary interactive buttons, prominent brand marks, and strong header typography. Creates immediate focal grounding against light surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#7091E6` - Periwinkle Blue):** Serves as the active electric accent. Applied to active tabs, hover states, selection outlines, toggles, and vivid progress indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#8697C4` - Steel Lavender):** Used for structural metadata, secondary icon fills, descriptive supporting labels, and calm status indicators.
- **Border & Outlines (`#ADBBDA` - Soft Periwinkle Mist):** Provides delicate containment lines, dividing rules, inactive indicator rings, and light tag backings.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#EDE8F5` - Pale Ice Periwinkle Tint):** The global foundation background. Prevents sterile visual fatigue while providing high contrast against elevated pure white containers.
- **Surface High (`#FFFFFF` - Pure Crisp White):** Dedicated to elevated cards, input fields, popovers, and primary focus panels.
- **Text Primary (`#1B254B`):** Deep midnight indigo body and heading color to guarantee absolute clarity under extreme light conditions without harsh carbon black tones.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across display, body, and UI labeling. With clean geometric proportions, deep apertures, and modern roundness, it produces immediate impact at scale while remaining legible at low resolutions or on projector lenses.

Key directives:
- **Numerical & Data Display:** Activate open-type font features `tnum` (tabular numerals) and `cv01` across all timers, counters, scores, and monetary values to ensure vertical column alignment without jitter during dynamic value changes.
- **Tightened Headings:** Display and headline levels enforce tight negative tracking (`-0.03em` to `-0.02em`) to bind titles into strong visual units.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Small labels (`label-md`, `label-sm`) default to uppercase with extended positive tracking (`0.04em` to `0.06em`) when paired with category indicators, badges, or table headers.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an 8pt-based fluid layout grid anchored inside structured containment envelopes:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Breakpoint 1280px+. 12 fluid columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, with 48px (`3rem`) viewport margins. Maximum content container width sits at 1440px to prevent excessive line lengths.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Breakpoint 768px - 1279px. 8 fluid columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, with 32px (`2rem`) margins. Content shifts from side-by-side matrices to stacked multi-span blocks.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Breakpoint <768px. 4 fluid columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, with 16px (`1rem`) outer margins. Toolbars and sub-metrics reflow to horizontal scroll carousels or stacked cards.

Component padding relies strictly on the `space-*` scale to maintain consistent internal rhythm: inputs and buttons leverage `space-sm` vertically and `space-md` horizontally, while dashboard cards employ `space-lg` to provide breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a dual-layer approach: **tonal surface stacking** combined with **tinted cool-ambient shadows**.

1. **Surface Hierarchy:**
   - **Base Canvas:** `#EDE8F5` (Pale Ice Periwinkle).
   - **Resting Cards & Containers:** `#FFFFFF` (Crisp White), layered over the pale canvas, creating immediate baseline contrast.
   - **Recessed / Secondary Panels:** `#EDE8F5` with a subtle 1px border of `#ADBBDA`.

2. **Shadow Architecture:**
   Instead of neutral grey shadows, shadows are tinted using the primary indigo spectrum (`rgb(61 82 160)`), preserving luminance without dirtiness.
   - **Level 0 (Flat):** 0px offset, 0px blur. Boundaries articulated exclusively by 1px borders (`#ADBBDA`).
   - **Level 1 (Card Default):** `0px 2px 8px -1px rgba(61, 82, 160, 0.08), 0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(61, 82, 160, 0.04)`. Outlined with a 1px `#ADBBDA` stroke.
   - **Level 2 (Hover & Active Surfaces):** `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(61, 82, 160, 0.12), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(61, 82, 160, 0.06)`. Border sharpens to `#7091E6`.
   - **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Menus):** `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(27, 37, 75, 0.18), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(27, 37, 75, 0.08)`. Surrounds surfaces with a 1px `#7091E6` hairline border.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates a Level 2 (`Rounded`) geometric vocabulary. This structure blends soft, friendly approachability with disciplined enterprise poise:

- **Base Radius (`rounded-md`, 0.5rem / 8px):** Form inputs, buttons, chips, nested widgets, and menu items.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Primary cards, content modules, presentation widgets, and table envelopes.
- **Structural Radius (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Dialog modals, hero presentation cards, and slide-in trays.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full`, 9999px):** Status badges, notification pips, avatars, and segmented filter pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#3D52A0`, label text `#FFFFFF` (Font weight 700). Hover state shifts background to `#7091E6` with a subtle elevation shift to Level 2. Active state uses `#2A3A73`. 
- **Secondary:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1.5px border `#3D52A0`, label text `#3D52A0`. Hover state fills with `#EDE8F5`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, label text `#3D52A0`. Hover background `#EDE8F5` at 50% opacity.
- **Focus Rings:** Unconditional 3px outline in `#7091E6` with a 2px offset in `#FFFFFF`.

### Inputs & Select Fields
- **Container:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1.5px border in `#ADBBDA`, text `#1B254B`. Internal padding: 12px 16px.
- **Hover:** Border shifts to `#8697C4`.
- **Focus:** Border transitions smoothly to `#7091E6` accompanied by a `0 0 0 3px rgba(112, 145, 230, 0.25)` focus halo.
- **Placeholder:** Styled in `#8697C4` for legible hinting.

### Chips & Badges
- **Informational Chip:** Background `#EDE8F5`, border 1px solid `#ADBBDA`, text `#3D52A0` (`label-md`).
- **Active / Filter Selected:** Background `#7091E6`, border 1px solid `#7091E6`, text `#FFFFFF`.
- **Pill Counters / Scores:** Bold tabular numerals (`tnum`), background `#3D52A0`, text `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards & Panels
- **Standard Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#ADBBDA`, shadow Level 1, border radius `16px` (`rounded-lg`). Padding `1.5rem`.
- **Interactive Card:** Transitions on hover to Level 2 shadow with border shifting to `#7091E6`.
- **Muted Container:** Background `#EDE8F5`, border 1px dashed `#8697C4`, internal padding `1rem`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Unchecked:** `#FFFFFF` fill with 2px border in `#8697C4`.
- **Checked:** Background `#3D52A0` with `#FFFFFF` checkmark indicator. Border color `#3D52A0`.
- **Focus:** 3px ring in `rgba(112, 145, 230, 0.4)`.

### Data Display & Metrics
- **Metric Value Callouts:** `display-lg` in `#1B254B` with `Plus Jakarta Sans` 800 weight, paired with micro-labels in `#8697C4` (`label-sm`).
- **Dividers:** 1px solid `#ADBBDA`, cleanly separating layout bands without dark visual clutter.
