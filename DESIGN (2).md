## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an ultra-premium, luminous broadcast aesthetic designed explicitly for collegiate tournament arena projectors, spectator video walls, and stage multi-screens. It balances high-intensity competitive focus with an uncompromising visual hierarchy that completely avoids traditional traffic-light signifiers (no green, red, or yellow). 

The visual style unites **High-Contrast Dark Minimalism** with **Luminescent Stage Architecture**. Built upon velvety obsidian, carbon, and deep violet-tinted onyx backdrops, interfaces project ultra-sharp geometric forms, razor-thin optic rims, and electric photonic glows. The emotional response is immediate and commanding: high-stakes academic rigor, modern cyber-athleticism, and absolute clarity from 30+ feet away under demanding stage ambient lighting.

## Colors

The color palette is engineered specifically to eliminate washout on stage projectors while completely excluding green, red, and yellow. All hues operate at peak chroma and high luminance against deep light-absorbing carbon substrates.

### Core Factions & Tournament Dialectics
- **Primary Brand / Team A (`#8B5CF6`)**: Electric Violet. Anchors tournament branding, confirmed answers, active lockout states, and Team A designation. Accented by `#A78BFA` for high-visibility perimeter highlights and `#7C3AED` for grounded active fills.
- **Secondary Accent / Team B (`#06B6D4`)**: Bright Cyan-Ice, pairing alongside Radiant Coral (`#FB923C`) for dual-team competitive contrast. Team B leverages high-candela cyan-ice wavelengths that slice through dark stage environments without color confusion.

### Status Architecture (Zero Green, Red, or Yellow)
- **Positive / Success State (`#22D3EE` & `#818CF8`)**: Electric Ice Cyan and Vivid Indigo signify valid responses, score awards, and correct question resolutions.
- **Warning / Miss State (`#E11D48` & `#BE185D`)**: Luminous Rose-Pink and Deep Plum indicate time penalties, incorrect buzzes, and critical timeouts.

### Projector-Safe Surfaces & Text
- `surface-obsidian` (`#0A0A0F`): Baseline master canvas that absorbs ambient venue spill.
- `surface-onyx` (`#12111A`): Primary structural bays and team pods.
- `surface-carbon` (`#1B1A26`): Elevated competitor modules and question displays.
- `surface-interactive` (`#262436`): Interactive cards, buzzer ready-tracks, and focused input wells.
- **Text Layers**: Pure optic white (`#FFFFFF`) and `#F1F5F9` dominate all data and typography tiers, ensuring maximum lumen reflection from long viewing ranges.

## Typography

The typography strategy pairs **Inter** for all editorial headlines, question prompts, and stage prompts with **JetBrains Mono** for numerical telemetry, clocks, and competitor indicators.

- **Inter** ensures crisp horizontal rhythm and large open counters that remain sharp when magnified on projector lenses. Question text utilizes `body-xl` (20px, semi-bold) or `headline-md` so contestants and spectators at 30+ feet can read without optical fatigue.
- **JetBrains Mono** enforces strictly tabular numbers (`tnum`) across `score-display`, round timers, and latency clocks to prevent lateral jitter during live numerical swings.
- Contrast protection: No text color falls below `#E2E8F0`. Low-contrast grays are barred to guarantee immediate 30-foot readability under stage lighting.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built for 16:9 large-format stage feeds and high-density operator decks.

- **Stage Projector & Video Wall (1920x1080 / 4K Mainstage)**: Employs a 12-column symmetrical fluid grid bounded by a safe `margin` of `2.5rem` (40px) to prevent overscan cropping on projector borders. Symmetrical split allocates 5 columns to Team Alpha, 2 columns to the Central Match Arena (clock, active round, buzzer lockout state), and 5 columns to Team B.
- **Tablet / Switcher Console (768px - 1024px)**: Transitions to an 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters, stacking contestant desks above answer logs.
- **Mobile Spectator Stream (<768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` margins. The match clock and score bay pin to the top header while team podiums stack vertically.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses luminous edge reflection and neon photoluminescence rather than diffused, muddy drop shadows that wash out under projection:

- **Surface 0 (Stage Horizon)**: Background `#0A0A0F`. Absolute light sink.
- **Surface 1 (Structural Arena Bays)**: `#12111A` framed by a 1px boundary stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- **Surface 2 (Elevated Match Podiums & Questions)**: `#1B1A26` with a crisp top-edge highlight (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)`) and an obsidian backing shadow (`0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`).
- **Surface 3 (Active Lockouts & Verified Answers)**: Backlit by volumetric glows:
  - Team A Active Glow: `0 0 36px -4px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.45)`
  - Team B Active Glow: `0 0 36px -4px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.45)`
  - Warning / Miss Flash: `0 0 36px -4px rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.45)`

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft** shape profile (`roundedness: 1`). Controlled 4px (`0.25rem`) and 8px (`0.5rem`) radiuses preserve a chiseled broadcast-console aesthetic.

- Standard buttons, telemetry chips, and input slots: `0.25rem` (4px).
- Mainstage question bays, team podium cards, and stage modules: `0.5rem` (8px).
- Full rounded pills (`9999px`) are exclusively reserved for match round badges, active buzzer lockouts, and live stream indicator chips.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Team A Action (Electric Violet)**: Background `#8B5CF6`, text `#0A0A0F` (heavy 700 weight), border none. Hover state intensifies to `#A78BFA` with a `0 0 24px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.5)` halo.
- **Team B Action (Bright Cyan)**: Background `#06B6D4`, text `#0A0A0F`, border none. Hover state intensifies to `#38BDF8` with a `0 0 24px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.5)` halo.
- **Neutral Console Trigger**: `#1B1A26` background, 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover transitions to `#262436` with a border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.35)`.

### Cards & Question Displays
- Structural foundation: `#1B1A26` with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Question cards feature an optic white `#FFFFFF` prompt in `headline-lg` or `body-xl`.
- Dynamic status states apply a 3px active boundary stroke (Violet for Team A, Cyan for Team B, Rose-Pink for Miss).

### Chips & Telemetry Tags
- All chips utilize uppercase `JetBrains Mono` (`label-caps`).
- **Success Chip**: Background `rgba(34, 211, 238, 0.15)`, border 1px solid `#22D3EE`, text `#22D3EE`.
- **Warning Chip**: Background `rgba(225, 29, 72, 0.15)`, border 1px solid `#E11D48`, text `#E11D48`.
- **Neutral Tag**: Background `#12111A`, border 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`, text `#E2E8F0`.

### Lists & Tournament Leaderboards
- High-contrast alternate rows (`#12111A` and `#1B1A26`) separated by 1px rules in `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Tabular figures in `#FFFFFF` `JetBrains Mono`. Top seeds highlighted with electric violet left-accent indicators.

### Inputs & Buzzer Switches
- Input slots utilize recessed `#0A0A0F` fills with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. Focus triggers a 1.5px border in `#8B5CF6` and an inner glow of `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`.
- Console Toggles: `#1B1A26` track, `#FFFFFF` thumb. Active Team A toggles illuminate in `#8B5CF6`; Team B toggles illuminate in `#06B6D4`.

### Projector Buzzer Lockout Banner
- Full-width hero banner pinned over the stage layout. When triggered, the board floods with the buzzing team's high-intensity accent (`#8B5CF6` or `#06B6D4`) and displays the player handle in pure `#0A0A0F`, creating unmistakable confirmation visible from anywhere in the auditorium.
